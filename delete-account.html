Delete Your PetBet Account

You can request permanent deletion of your PetBet account and associated data at any time, whether or not you still have the app installed.

How to request deletion

Send an email to petbetapp@gmail.com with the subject line "Account Deletion Request," using the same email address you registered with PetBet.

We will confirm receipt and process your deletion request within 30 days.

What gets deleted

  • Your account information (email, profile data)
  • Pet photos and analysis history you've uploaded
  • Symptom descriptions submitted to Virtual Vet
  • Lost Pet Registry entries you've created
  • Subscription and billing records tied to your account (subject to what Stripe and applicable law require us to retain for financial record-keeping)

What may be retained

We may retain limited information where required by law (e.g., transaction records for tax or fraud-prevention purposes) for a limited period after deletion, in accordance with our Privacy Policy.

Questions about this process can be sent to petbetapp@gmail.com.
