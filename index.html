FAQ

What people ask

Does PetBet replace a real veterinarian?

No. Virtual Vet helps you understand what might be going on and when to be concerned, but we always recommend visiting a veterinarian for real diagnoses and treatment.

How accurate is the age and breed detection?

Our AI looks at teeth, eyes, coat, and body proportions to give the closest estimate possible. With mixed breeds, it may show more than one possibility.

Is my photo and data safe?

Yes. We never sell or share your information with third parties. Your photos are used only for analysis, and you can delete your account and data anytime.

Does it work with animals that aren't dogs or cats?

Breed, age, and mood analysis is optimized for dogs and cats. The lost pet registry works with any animal.
